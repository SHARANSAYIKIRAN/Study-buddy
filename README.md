# 🖤 Study Buddy

### ˚₊‧꒰ა Your little corner of focus, learning & growth ໒꒱ ‧₊˚

A cozy, AI-powered study space designed to make learning feel a little less overwhelming and a lot more fun. 📚☁️

Study Buddy brings your PDFs, AI study assistant, focus timer, and study progress together in one place — so you can focus on learning, one little step at a time.

---

## 🌱 What makes it special?

📖 **Your Personal Study Room**  
Open your PDFs, explore your notes, and study without the clutter.

🤖 **AI That Studies With You**  
Ask questions, simplify difficult concepts, and get help understanding your study materials.

🧠 **Your AI, Your Choice**  
Choose your preferred AI provider and model, with support designed for Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Ollama, and OpenRouter.

⏳ **A Little Focus Goes a Long Way**  
Use a floating circular focus timer to stay on track. Pause, resume, and keep it close while reading your PDFs.

✅ **Tiny Tasks, Big Progress**  
Create your own checklist or let AI turn your study material into manageable portions. Tick things off as you go!

📊 **Watch Yourself Grow**  
Track your daily study time, weekly progress, goals, and study streaks.

🖤 **A Dark Space to Think**  
A clean, pure-black interface with subtle accents, designed to keep distractions out of your way.

## 🚀 Getting started

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone YOUR_REPOSITORY_URL
cd study-buddy-main
```

Start a local development server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 3000
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

Configure your AI provider and API credentials in Settings to use the corresponding AI features.

> 💡 Keep API keys private. Never commit secret credentials to a public repository.
